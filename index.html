    03The craft

    Four steps, no hurry.

    Leather rewards patience. These are the moments every piece passes through before it is ready to leave the bench.

    01/ 04

    1. 01

      Selection

      Chosen by hand

      Each hide is unrolled in daylight and read like a map. We keep the ones with a close, even grain, and mark every flaw before a knife comes near.

    2. 02

      Cutting

      Cut along the grain

      Pieces are cut one at a time, following the direction of the grain, so that every part stretches and settles the same way.

    3. 03

      Stitching

      Two needles, one thread

      Saddle-stitched with waxed thread, pulled tight stitch by stitch. If one thread ever breaks, the seam still holds.

    4. 04

      Finishing

      Finished slowly

      Edges are burnished, the leather is fed with wax and polished by hand, until it takes on a soft, deep shine.

    Manifesto

    We believe a good thing should get better with age. So we choose leather that darkens, softens and remembers — and we make every piece slowly enough to be worn for years, mended when it needs it, and one day handed on.
