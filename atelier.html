02Made to order

From a conversation to your door.

For shoes made to your measure, a bag drawn around what you carry, or a piece that does not exist yet. Five unhurried steps — and you are part of every one.

Timings depend on the piece and the leather; we agree them with you at the first conversation.

Book a consultation
  1. 01

    Consultation

    We sit down together — in the boutique or on a call — and talk about what you want to wear, carry and keep.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Feet are measured and traced; a bag is drawn around what it must hold. Nothing is left to guesswork.

  3. 03

    Select

    You choose the leather from the hides themselves, then the thread, the lining and the finish.

  4. 04

    Craft

    Cut, stitched and finished by hand, at the pace leather asks for. We keep you posted along the way.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Your piece is fitted and handed over in person, or sent to you wrapped — with its care notes.

    More in client services

    Our bags and small leather goods — wallets, card holders, belts and travel pieces. Look for “Personalise it” on a product page, or begin right here.

    Up to three characters: the Latin letters A–Z, the digits 0–9 and a full stop, in classic serif or Roman capitals. If you have something else in mind, tell us at an appointment.

    Foil lays a fine film of gold or silver into the pressed letters. A blind deboss uses no foil at all — only the impression in the leather, which deepens quietly as the leather ages.

    Personalisation adds $25 to the price of the piece, whichever foil and lettering you choose.
